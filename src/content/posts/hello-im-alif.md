I'm Alif, a freelance developer and CSE student at Daffodil International University in Dhaka. I like systems more than screens: how the data is stored, who is allowed to do what, and what happens when something goes wrong.

My first real client project is Alez POS, a point-of-sale system for Yaucha Food & Beverage. Besides client work, I take part in hackathons and do research on traffic accident prediction with a small team. I am also preparing for an exchange semester at IIT Bombay that starts at the end of this year.

## What I'll write about

- **Tech.** Notes on system design, offline-first software and building with AI tools. I can read code well, and I use AI to help write most of it, so I will be honest about both.
- **Travel.** Short notes from places I have been in Bangladesh and Malaysia.
- **Tech and economy.** How technology companies and markets shape what we build, written as opinion and not as advice.
- **My take.** Plain thoughts on building a career in software from Bangladesh.

Some posts here are explainers and others are opinions, and I will say which is which. If you have a project in mind, [get in touch](/contact).
